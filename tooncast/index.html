Questions

Will the character look exactly like me?

It looks like a low-poly 3D version of you: your skin tone, hair, glasses, beard and outfit colours. That's the charm of the style. It isn't a photo-real portrait.

Do I need to know animation or code?

No. You answer questions, approve a preview and give topics. Claude does the building, animating and rendering.

How long does one episode take?

A few minutes of your time. Claude then works on its own while the episode renders, usually well under an hour on a normal computer.

Can my business use it?

Yes. It's built on Remotion, which is free for individuals and teams of up to 3 people. Bigger companies need their own Remotion company licence.
